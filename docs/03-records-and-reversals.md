## Open checks (raised on a heating-controls wiring course, Oct 2026)
| # | Check | Why | Options if confirmed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Radiator zone valves in manual override:** how far open are they? | A latched manual lever may hold the valve only partly open, restricting flow to the radiator circuits | Re-power the heads on a permanent live (the motor drives fully open and releases the latch) **or** replace the valve bodies (needs a drain-down) |
| 2 | **Pump overrun / minimum-flow path:** is there an automatic bypass (in the boiler or the pipework)? | With TRV heads on every radiator and the zone valves shut, the overrun pump may have nowhere to circulate | Confirm from the boiler manual and pipework; if absent, fit an automatic bypass valve or leave one radiator (e.g. a towel rail) without a TRV head |
| 3 | **Unvented cylinder high-limit cut-outs** still in series with each cylinder's valve circuit under Evohome | G3 safety requirement | Trace and test with a G3-qualified engineer; restore the series connection if bypassed |
